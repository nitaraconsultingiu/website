Who we are

Global perspective.
Grounded thinking.

Nitara is an undergraduate consulting organization built around a simple idea: ambitious students and ambitious organizations grow faster together.

Every semester, interdisciplinary teams work directly with international startups, businesses, and NGOs, bringing fresh perspective to consequential questions.
